# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local customers discovering The Monkey's Captain food stall and considering its menu or event catering.

## Product Purpose

Present the restaurant with immediate appetite appeal and guide visitors toward exploring the menu or booking the stall for an event.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, and energetic, with a street-food character grounded in the existing monkey-chef identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid quiet fine-dining minimalism, generic restaurant templates, and polished corporate styling that weakens the lively street-food personality.

## Design Principles

- Make the food and brand character instantly recognizable.
- Use strong, poster-like hierarchy rather than delicate decoration.
- Keep calls to action direct and easy to spot.
- Preserve the established dark, warm, high-energy visual direction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional accessibility requirements are in scope for the current visual pass.
